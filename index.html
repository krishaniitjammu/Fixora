Questions

How can you diagnose without seeing the appliance?

Most appliance faults show clear symptoms. Our experts ask a few targeted questions and narrow it down to the likely cause. The technician confirms it on site before any work starts, and you approve the final price.

What if the fault turns out to be something else?

You get a revised itemised quote on the spot. If you decide not to go ahead, you pay only the ₹149 inspection fee.

Why is Fixora cheaper than a local shop?

We source parts in bulk directly from distributors and charge a fixed, published labour rate for each repair, so there is no room for a mark-up on the day.

Can I buy only the spare part?

Yes. Every part is sold separately with delivery in 24 to 48 hours. You can add a technician visit later if you need help fitting it.

Is there a warranty?

Every repair carries a 90-day service warranty, and parts carry their own warranty of up to 12 months.
